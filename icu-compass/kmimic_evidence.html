Cohort selection and data coverage

These are code-based candidates, pending confirmation of hospital unit definitions. Generic ICU, 1ICU, 2ICU, CCU and OP_FLAG alone are not used to infer a surgical ICU. Different hospitals' subject IDs are not treated as the same person.

Only a small sample is loaded into this replay, not the entire candidate cohort. Diagnosis, GCS, ventilation and vasopressor status are not mapped. Missing support status does not mean treatment was absent.

Method and references

AgentMD · Nature Communications (2025) motivates checking eligibility, executing a calculator and explaining its inputs. This prototype implements a deterministic qSOFA reference calculation. It does not run or reproduce AgentMD or its LLM-generated urgency and severity scores.

qSOFA · Sepsis-3, JAMA (2016), Box 4: one point each for RR ≥22/min, SBP ≤100 mmHg and altered mentation. Intended context: adults with suspected infection, particularly outside ICU. This ICU replay does not use qSOFA to diagnose sepsis or confirm an emergency.

Replay thresholds are existing app settings: HR <40 or >140, MAP <60, SpO₂ <88, RR >35. They are not formulas validated by the AgentMD paper. No treatment recommendation or mortality probability is inferred.
